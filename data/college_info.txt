About Us
P.T.Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker College of Engineering & Technology is the outcome of our great Philanthropist, late Shri. P. T.Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker (1829 - 1874) with a sole aim of providing Technical Education to the poor and downtrodden people beside other social and religious activities.
The College is situated in Oovery, 8 kms from Kancheepuram and 18 kms from Arakkonam. A private, self-financed Institution, affiliated to Anna University, approved by AICTE.

History
The college is named after the great philanthropist Late Shri P.T. Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker (1829-1874), who dedicated his life to the upliftment of society through education. Established in 1998, the institution has grown into one of the premier engineering colleges in Tamil Nadu.

▸

Mission
▸
To nurture in the students, professional and ethical values, and to instil in them a spirit of innovation and entrepreneurship.
▸
To encourage in the students a desire for higher learning and research, to equip them to face the global challenges.
▸
To provide opportunities for students to get the needed additional skills to make them industry ready.
▸
To implicate with academic, industrial and government entities to attain collaborative research.
▸
To solve the real life technical problems and to render the Consultancy-Extension service to the industries.

Early Life & Family
P.T. Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker was born in the year 1829. His father, Perumal Naicker, on whom the Government had conferred the title "Sirdar Bahadur", was a Subedar-Major in the Madras Army. His mother Theivayanaiammal, towards whom her son showed a rare respect and devotion, was very pious, orthodox and conservative.
Even as a child, Chengalvaraya Naicker was noted for his sweet nature, devotion to God and his parents, and kindness to all around him. As he grew up he developed a keen interest in business which led him to adopt trade and commerce as his profession.
Business Career
At his father's death, Chengalvaraya Naicker, who was thirty years of age, set up a business of his own and soon found himself a leader of the Indian trading community in Madras. He became a Dubash of Messrs. Shand and Co. and soon amassed a great fortune. He was very capable and hence successful in business.
Philanthropy & The Great Famine of 1857
His heart melted at the sight of the poor and the needy. During the great famine of 1857, his house became a regular relief camp where the necessities of life were distributed to all those who were in need of them, irrespective of their caste and creed.
His favourite resort was the great temple in Triplicane. Under his patronage a Sabah was founded with the object of editing and publishing the sacred books of the Vaishnavas.
The Historic Will
A scheme for ensuring the perpetual continuance of his benevolent charities had been engaging his attention for a number of years and finally resulted in a will dated 1870 (slightly modified by a codicil in 1878) bequeathing the whole of his estate to religious and charitable purposes.
Will Provisions:
Estate administered by a body of Trustees
Modest allowances for poor students of his community
Specific religious offerings in temples
His choultry at Oovery to be permanently maintained
Sadavarthi for Bhairagi pilgrims at Kanchipuram
Balance for Orphanage and technical education
The Revolutionary Name "P.T.Lee"
One of the most notable things in his will is his wish to include initials "P.T.Lee" before his name. He wanted both his father
Perumal Naicker (P)
and his mother
Theivayanaiammal (T)
In recognition of his remarkable contributions, P.T. Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker was honored by the British, who bestowed upon him the name ‘Lee’ as a mark of distinction. The name was not inherited, but granted — a tribute to his service and the respect he earned.
This was a revolutionary act demonstrating his disagreement with the prevalent male chauvinism of excluding a mother's name in one's initials.
Final Years
The great soul passed away in 1874, at the early age of 45, leaving behind a legacy of compassion, philanthropy, and visionary thinking that continues to inspire generations. We can confidently say that P.T. Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker lived and thought centuries ahead of his times.
Legacy Continues
Today, P.T.Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker College of Engineering and Technology stands as a living monument to his vision, providing technical education to thousands of students and fulfilling his dream of empowering the poor and downtrodden through education.

About Us
P.T.Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker College of Engineering & Technology is the outcome of our great Philanthropist, late Shri. P. T.Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker (1829 - 1874) with a sole aim of providing Technical Education to the poor and downtrodden people beside other social and religious activities.
The College is situated in Oovery, 8 kms from Kancheepuram and 18 kms from Arakkonam. A private, self-financed Institution, affiliated to Anna University, approved by AICTE.

History
The college is named after the great philanthropist Late Shri P.T. Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker (1829-1874), who dedicated his life to the upliftment of society through education. Established in 1998, the institution has grown into one of the premier engineering colleges in Tamil Nadu.

To nurture in the students, professional and ethical values, and to instil in them a spirit of innovation and entrepreneurship.

To solve the real life technical problems and to render the Consultancy-Extension service to the industries.

Early Life & Family
P.T. Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker was born in the year 1829. His father, Perumal Naicker, on whom the Government had conferred the title "Sirdar Bahadur", was a Subedar-Major in the Madras Army. His mother Theivayanaiammal, towards whom her son showed a rare respect and devotion, was very pious, orthodox and conservative.
Even as a child, Chengalvaraya Naicker was noted for his sweet nature, devotion to God and his parents, and kindness to all around him. As he grew up he developed a keen interest in business which led him to adopt trade and commerce as his profession.
Business Career
At his father's death, Chengalvaraya Naicker, who was thirty years of age, set up a business of his own and soon found himself a leader of the Indian trading community in Madras. He became a Dubash of Messrs. Shand and Co. and soon amassed a great fortune. He was very capable and hence successful in business.
Philanthropy & The Great Famine of 1857
His heart melted at the sight of the poor and the needy. During the great famine of 1857, his house became a regular relief camp where the necessities of life were distributed to all those who were in need of them, irrespective of their caste and creed.
His favourite resort was the great temple in Triplicane. Under his patronage a Sabah was founded with the object of editing and publishing the sacred books of the Vaishnavas.
The Historic Will
A scheme for ensuring the perpetual continuance of his benevolent charities had been engaging his attention for a number of years and finally resulted in a will dated 1870 (slightly modified by a codicil in 1878) bequeathing the whole of his estate to religious and charitable purposes.
Will Provisions:
Estate administered by a body of Trustees
Modest allowances for poor students of his community
Specific religious offerings in temples
His choultry at Oovery to be permanently maintained
Sadavarthi for Bhairagi pilgrims at Kanchipuram
Balance for Orphanage and technical education
The Revolutionary Name "P.T.Lee"
One of the most notable things in his will is his wish to include initials "P.T.Lee" before his name. He wanted both his father
Perumal Naicker (P)
and his mother
Theivayanaiammal (T)
In recognition of his remarkable contributions, P.T. Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker was honored by the British, who bestowed upon him the name ‘Lee’ as a mark of distinction. The name was not inherited, but granted — a tribute to his service and the respect he earned.
This was a revolutionary act demonstrating his disagreement with the prevalent male chauvinism of excluding a mother's name in one's initials.
Final Years
The great soul passed away in 1874, at the early age of 45, leaving behind a legacy of compassion, philanthropy, and visionary thinking that continues to inspire generations. We can confidently say that P.T. Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker lived and thought centuries ahead of his times.
Legacy Continues
Today, P.T.Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker College of Engineering and Technology stands as a living monument to his vision, providing technical education to thousands of students and fulfilling his dream of empowering the poor and downtrodden through education.

About the Department
NBA Accredited | Research Center
Recognized for excellence in Electrical & Electronics Engineering education
The Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering was established in the year 2001 with an intake of 60 students. The department is accredited by NBA and recognized as a research center by Anna University. The curriculum is designed to provide students with a strong foundation in electrical machines, power systems, control systems, power electronics, and renewable energy technologies.
The department has state-of-the-art laboratories with modern equipment and simulation software. The faculty members are highly qualified with Ph.D. from reputed institutions and have extensive research experience in power systems, electrical drives, and renewable energy. The department has strong industry collaborations with leading power sector companies for internships, projects, and placements.

About the Department
The Department of Science and Humanities has been functioning since the inception of the college in
2001
. It serves as the foundation for all engineering students, providing strong fundamentals in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and English. The department plays a crucial role in shaping the analytical and scientific temper of future engineers.
With a team of highly qualified faculty members, many holding Ph.D. degrees from reputed institutions, the department ensures that students receive excellent training in basic sciences. The department also offers value-added courses and coaching for competitive examinations like GATE, TANCET, and campus placement tests.
Developing the moral behaviour among the students, utilizing the human resources and coordinating academic responsibilities are the exclusive potentials of the department. The department regularly organizes workshops, symposiam and conferences in emerging areas for the benefit of the students, researchers, faculty members and industrialists.

Welcome Note 2026
Science & Humanities
Dear Students,
It is my privilege to welcome you to the Department of Science and Humanities at P.T.Lee CNCET. As the foundation department of our institution, we take immense pride in shaping the first-year engineering students and providing them with the strong fundamental knowledge necessary for their engineering journey.
Our department comprises four vibrant disciplines: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and English. Each discipline is staffed by highly qualified faculty members who are not only excellent teachers but also active researchers. We believe that a strong foundation in basic sciences is essential for every engineer to understand complex engineering concepts and innovate.
"Our goal is to nurture analytical thinking, scientific curiosity, and effective communication skills that will serve our students throughout their professional careers."
We regularly organize guest lectures, workshops, and bridge courses to help students transition smoothly from school to engineering education. Our faculty also provide coaching for competitive examinations and campus placements. The department has published over 85 research papers in reputed journals and has completed 8 funded research projects.
I invite you to explore the opportunities our department offers and join us in building a strong foundation for your engineering career.

Thousands of books are relate to Science and Humanities, Engineering & Technology, Management, National & International Journals and a large number of magazines. The Library is modern, computerized and well stocked with current books, periodicals, management videos, CDs and other learning resources. Students can also use this room for quiet self study. In addition to that P.T.Lee students have access to an exhaustive e-library permitting access to leading journals in engineering and business magazines.
Collection Highlights
The library has developed an excellent collection of books, journals and non-book materials in science, Engineering, Technology, Humanities, Social sciences and management. It maintains separate collections of Reference books, Bound volume of Journals technical reports thesis, videos cassettes, compacts discs and Microforms.

Rules & Regulations
General Rules
Library membership of all Teaching staff, Non teaching staff and students are the member of the Central Library
All members shall sign in the register provided at the entrance
Under no circumstances, personal books, less and other articles will be allowed inside the library
Strict silence shall always be maintained in the library
Any marking or writing in the books by the members is strictly prohibited
Members are solely responsible for any damage caused to the books
Misbehavior will lead to cancellation of membership
Use of mobile phones not permitted inside the Library
Fine of Rs.1 per day for overdue books
Lost books - replace or pay triple cost
Library Cards
Under Graduate Students - 4 Cards
Teaching Staff - 5 Cards
Non – teaching staff – 3 cards
Student members can obtain membership cards from the library only on producing their identity cards
Library cards are not transferable
Only one book can be borrowed on each card
Members are responsible for all the books borrowed on their cards
Loss of Library cards should be reported to the Librarian in writing
Duplicate cards will be issued on payment of penalty of Rs.50/- per card
Issue and Return
Books other than reference books and periodicals will be issued to student members for a period of 15 days. For teachers, the period of issue is three months
Two renewals of books will be allowed provided there are no reservations
Before leaving the issue counter, members must satisfy themselves that books issued to them are in good condition
Books which are in demand may be issued for a short period
Absence from college will not be permitted as an excuse for delay in returning the books
